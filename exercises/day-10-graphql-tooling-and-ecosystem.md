# Day 10: GraphQL Tooling and Ecosystem (Project-Based)

## Topics
- Using GraphQL Playground and Apollo DevTools with Next.js
- Generating TypeScript types from your schema
- Documenting your GraphQL schema

## Exercises
1. Use your project's GraphQL Playground UI and Apollo Client DevTools to inspect queries, mutations, and cache. Take screenshots and note useful features.
2. Set up a codegen tool (e.g., GraphQL Code Generator) to generate TypeScript types for your queries and mutations based on your schema. Integrate it into your workflow and verify type safety in your hooks/components.
3. (Bonus) Generate and publish schema documentation (e.g., using graphql-markdown or similar) for your API. Link to it from your README.
